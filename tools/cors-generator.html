Why not just use *

With credentials enabled, * means any site on the internet can make authenticated requests to your API on behalf of a logged-in user. An explicit allowlist of your own domains closes that door while keeping your real front-ends working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Access-Control-Allow-Origin: * dangerous?

It is risky whenever cookies or credentials are involved, because it lets any origin make authenticated calls to your API. Use an explicit allowlist of domains you control instead.

How do I allow multiple origins in CORS?

Check the incoming Origin header against a list of allowed domains and echo it back only if it matches, rather than returning a wildcard. This tool generates exactly that for Express, Nginx and PHP.
